*Lawful evil gnome cult leader (age 33)*

A forest gnome taking advantage of poor living conditions to extort labor from a mass of gnomes who have nowhere to go. He has a plain look to him, hiding his insidious nature. With his authority as bishop, he is able to hoard funding both from the church and from his followers to live a comfortable lifestyle. 

**(DECEASED)**
Executed by [[Saintess Lucantor du Promade III]] during an ill-thought blackmail attempt.
