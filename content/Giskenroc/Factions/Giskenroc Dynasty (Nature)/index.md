---
title: Gisken Dynasty (Nature)
---
The Gisken Dynasty is the ruling authority within Giskenroc. They oversee the land, create legislation, and are responsible for the well-being of its citizens. Generally, the royal family and its council are largely hands-off of the empire, leading to a very diverse population with several interests. As far as royal families go, the Gisken are kind and generous.

The royal family and the council of Trebytheus are composed of pureblooded elves. Together they vote on policies proposed by the public. For the last 500 years, its been impossible for a common citizen to join the dynasty. The only method thus far has been through marriage, which is rare for non-fey citizens. 

Its not clear what the Gisken Dynasty has to gain from being rather indulgent leaders. Most of the population has accepted them as part of the common folk; they're people like them who are doing their best to cultivate a future. If they have anything to hide, and they definitely do, it likely isn't important.
