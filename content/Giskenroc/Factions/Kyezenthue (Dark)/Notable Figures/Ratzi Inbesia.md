---
title: Ratzi Inbesia
---
*Lawful neutral halfling ringleader (age 36)*

The most ostentatious halfling you've ever seen. A large fur coat, more jewelry seen than skin, and a sick pair of shades. He is the ringleader of a moderately sized Kyezenthue fight club. Despite his business, he's a surprisingly nice guy. He'll hook people up with jobs and do some small favors, but he always has a personal reason for doing so. Ratzi is a great first step toward the criminal underworld.
