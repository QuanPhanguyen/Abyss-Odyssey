*True neutral high-elf council-member (age unknown)*

Sharp features with soft expressions, a golden corset with beaming sigils as an augment, and a tipped helm. Despite his frightening naval commander appearance, Nabon Trebytheus is one of the most non-violent elves you'll ever meet. His focus lies entirely within archeology and history keeping. This isn't to say he isn't threatening. As one of the nation's most powerful spellcasters, he finds other means to keep the system in check. 

At least, so it seems.
