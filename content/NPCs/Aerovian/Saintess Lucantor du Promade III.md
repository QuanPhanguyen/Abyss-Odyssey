*Chaotic good human saintess (age 17)*

One of two human children adopted by [[Current Cardinal|Rh'aap Blackhide]]. Trained from an early age to retain all of the church's teachings, Lucantor is the younger of the twin sisters. Compared to [[Giskenroc/Factions/Congregant of Aerovar (Light)/Notable Figures/Saintess Lucille du Promade III]], she is brash, brazen, and rowdy. She does not mind her manners, and she speaks aloud whatever comes to mind. Her accomplishments are few, although it is evident she is quite powerful with her sheltered military upbringing.

Lucantor has short blonde hair, styled in a way no different than her sister. It can be seen as both tomboy-ish and elegant, which makes it difficult for many to distinguish the two from each other. Because the two of them are typically assigned to pious duties, they are nearly always wearing white, silken robes with gilded crests. 

Lucantor will likely not be chosen to take up the mantle of Aero because of her rambunctious disposition ill-suited for politics, although it is still up in the air. Either sibling could rise up to become the head of the Congregant of Aerovar.
