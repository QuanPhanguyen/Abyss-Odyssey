*Lawful good human saintess (age 17)*

One of two human children adopted by [[Current Cardinal|Rh'aap Blackhide]]. Trained from an early age to retain all of the church's teachings, Lucille is the older of the twin sisters. Compared to [[Giskenroc/Factions/Congregant of Aerovar (Light)/Notable Figures/Saintess Lucantor du Promade III]], she is soft, well-spoken, and kind. She's careful to always show a positive face, and she never speaks ill of anyone. Her accomplishments are few, although it is evident she is quite powerful with her sheltered military upbringing.

Lucille has short blonde hair, styled in a way no different than her sister. It can be seen as both elegant and tomboy-ish, which makes it difficult for many to distinguish the two from each other. Because the two of them are typically assigned to pious duties, they are nearly always wearing white, silken robes with gilded crests. 

Lucille will likely be chosen to take up the mantle of Aero because of her gentle nature and silvery tongue, well-suited for politics, although it is still up in the air. Either sibling could rise up to become the head of the Congregant of Aerovar.
