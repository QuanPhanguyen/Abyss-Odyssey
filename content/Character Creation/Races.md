title: Species
---
As a general rule of thumb, this kingdom won't be seeing too many exotic or unique species until later on in the campaign, since it's quite literally a closed border nation at the moment. However, this doesn't mean that everyone within the kingdom will be human. 

I'll list below some common species and their background information in this campaign. If you don't see your desired species listed below, don't worry about it. There isn't background knowledge required for that species.

<br> <br>

### Dwarves
As a species, Dwarves are best suited working in the forge, causing them generally to shift toward the [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/Factions/Kyezenthue (Dark)/index|Kyezenthue]]. This is not absolute.

### Elves/Fairies/Centaurs/Changeling/Satyr
The fey compose the [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/Factions/Giskenroc Dynasty (Nature)/index|Royal Family]] and are the founders of Giskenroc. No matter what sort of fey creature you play, you should have some form of connection with the dynasty. 

### Warforged
Warforged in this campaign 100% originate from [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/Factions/Kyezenthue (Dark)/index|Kyezenthue]] as of now. Your character should have some connection with them.
