Artificer - The biggest form of magic technology hybrids come in the form of [[Homebrew/Augment/index|Augments]], so it's likely you have some ties to the [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/Factions/Kyezenthue (Dark)/index|Kyezenthue]]. 

Barbarian - Barbarians could potentially be aligned with any of the three factions, but it's more likely they're factionless.

Bard - Culture either comes from those who can afford it or those who can't afford anything. In that regard, Bards will either be tied in some way to the [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/Factions/Giskenroc Dynasty (Nature)/index|Royal Family]], the [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/Factions/Congregant of Aerovar (Light)/index|Congregant]], or factionless.

Cleric - The main religion in Giskenroc has to do with the [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/Factions/Congregant of Aerovar (Light)/index|Congregant]]. You're completely welcome to realize this faith as you see fit, or create a minor one of your own.
