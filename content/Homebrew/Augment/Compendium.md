### [Giskenroc Augments](https://homebrewery.naturalcrit.com/share/qiw6qqKAB8hG)

A list of everyday Augments obtainable within [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/index|Giskenroc]] for practically no gold. As they're the weakest form of Augment (man-made and low-grade), they have no significant cost to the user.
