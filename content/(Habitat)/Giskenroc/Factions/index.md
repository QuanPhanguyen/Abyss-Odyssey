title: Factions
---
Our story takes place within a barren, grim, dark landscape of blackstone and cobble, where a kingdom titled Giskenroc shoddily stands. It is a kingdom whose technology has surpassed all other societal factors, a fact easily surveyable at a glance with how careening and violent it is. For forty hours, the empire is swallowed by darkness. At the end of this timeframe, one of the gateways to leave the biome deep, deep in the sky lines up with other layers and sunlight appears for a brief fifteen minutes.  

Life within Giskenroc is perilous for those who wish to live even a remotely modest lifestyle. Citizens can survive off of menial tasks and everyday handiwork, but in order to obtain necessities like shelter or provide for their families, they must be prepared to put themselves in danger. They can aid in dubious experiments, work in unsafe factories, or put everything on the line to explore other biomes. 

Three major factions drive the empire's direction as of this moment. [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/Factions/Congregant of Aerovar (Light)/index|The Congregant of Aerovar]], a church focused on the sun, the [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/Factions/Giskenroc Dynasty (Nature)/index|Giskenroc Dynasty]], the royal line who founded the kingdom, and the [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/Factions/Kyezenthue (Dark)/index|Kyezenthue]], a coalition of researchers and blacksmiths dedicated to ancient technology.
