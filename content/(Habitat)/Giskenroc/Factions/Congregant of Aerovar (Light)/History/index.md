---
title: History
---
The Congregant of Aerovar was the leading faction in the early days of the Giskenroc dynasty. Their charismatic approach to rallying common folk garnered them a strong reputation, and before technology developed and became fully understood, the people relied on the Sun to obtain resources. 

In the decades 220 LM to 230 LM, Aerovar's political power began to wane. The advancement of ancient tools and productions led people to rely less on the sun's already miniscule presence in the land. Sensing this, the Aero used their remaining influence to attempt a risky expedition in order to recoup their declining reputation. They funded everything they could into a space crusade, hoping to obtain something by sending missionaries toward the source of the light. All of the empire's research facilities and scientists pivoted onto a single goal, in a monumental and unrepeatable unison. Even the religion's greatest adversaries aided in the prospect. This was a gamble by the Aerovar, and them losing meant their faction crumbling to obscurity. But if the congregant could obtain anything, even a second-hand account of what lay beyond the hole in the sky, they could spin it into a message powerful enough to grant them influence for the foreseeable future.

Beyond anyone's expectations, something else occurred. The ship never returned. A legendary-grade augment, now proclaimed the "Aerovian Judgement," miraculously crashed through the church's roof and pierced the main halls. This single incident has caused the Aerovian Congregant to remain an influential faction within the empire to this day, although never to the degree they were before. The ancient technology used to power the vessel and the societal frenzy to push for the expedition would never return despite the congregant's best efforts. 

(Some conspiracists believe this event to be faked, although there is no proof of this.)
