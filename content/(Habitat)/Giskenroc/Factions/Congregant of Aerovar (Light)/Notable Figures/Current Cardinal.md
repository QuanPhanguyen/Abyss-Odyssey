---
title: Aero Rh'aap
---
The current Aero of the Congregant of Aerovar. Erase all images of godly figures in your mind, and imagine instead a large orc whose frame is as wide as an ancient temple's column and as tall as a wyvern. In his youngest years, he was a disciplined military man, leading expeditions to some of the most dangerous biomes the kingdom has ever charted. 

All of his treasures and knowledge were donated to the Congregant. He quickly rose in status and became a contester for the one who'd receive the church's most prized possession, the Aerovian Judgement. With his merits, he became a strong contention for the head of the Congregant, a position which was notorious at the time for being decided off of connections. After a long deliberation from the chambers of the Congregant with every Cardinal included, Rh'aap was sworn in to Aero by majority vote.

Rh'aap holds a neutral stance on most policies, and focuses mostly on the well-being of the common folk. His strongest advocation is toward the loosening of expedition requirements, as he believes it to be a solid gateway into a better life. Rh'aap has two adopted human daughters, the eldest of which he is raising to take his place as Aero.
