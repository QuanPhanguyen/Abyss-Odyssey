---
title: Congregant of Aerovar (Light)
---
The Congregant of Aerovar is the major religion within Giskenroc. They worship the beaming light that shines every forty-two hours. Minor variants of their faith exists throughout the kingdom, since there is no centralized mantra detailing their core beliefs. Generally speaking, the faith asks for you to participate as much as you can in the journey to the Sun, as it will bring eternal paradise. Practitioners are known to be superstitious, kind, and tribal-like.
