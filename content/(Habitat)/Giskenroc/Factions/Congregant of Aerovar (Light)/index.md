---
title: Congregant of Aerovar (Light)
---
The Congregant of Aerovar is the major religion within Giskenroc. They worship the beaming light that shines every forty-two hours. Minor variants of their faith exists throughout the kingdom, since there is no centralized mantra detailing their core beliefs. Generally speaking, the faith asks for you to participate as much as you can in the journey to the Sun, as it will bring eternal paradise. Practitioners are known to be superstitious, kind, and tribal-like.

Being an official within the Congregant grants you the same political power as holding a position within the government. Although the Aerovar have no direct responsibilities regarding the empire's infrastructure, resource supply, or social/fiscal policy, they keep their skin the game by influencing those who do. Belief is a powerful thing, and the Aerovar sway it to fulfill their gains. For the morally corrupt, this means obtaining benefits they don't deserve. For the truly pious, this means shaping the blessed future of the kingdom in the way they see fit.

Priests make up the lowest layer of the hierarchal pyramid, followed by bishops and cardinals of various degrees of ranking, and topped off by Aero, the figure head. Their duties involve mostly ceremonious cultural events, such as hosting holidays, leading mass, and ushering in the bidaily sunlight.
