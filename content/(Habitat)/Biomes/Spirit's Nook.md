The Spirit's Nook is a recent discovery made off of the loosening restrictions of biome exploration. It's now become a popular alternative to Dezerekt, as explorers and adventurers from Giskenroc drool for the idea of fresh resources to plunder. Materials from this biome contain a unique energy signature ripe for augment creation. This biome contains massive flora, notably colossal trees with alcoves and vines. Exploration contains great verticality. A bright fog covers the land. It makes it difficult to vantage distances further than a couple hundred meters out. A mossy, dirt floor holds many different materials for harvest, so most citizens canvas the lower levels. 

Most worryingly is the collection of named beasts which roam this land. Any creatures native to this land subsist on large batches of tiny food sources, like grass or wisps. They mostly hunt intruders for sport. It's rare to encounter any of these creatures, but the experience is nearly always fatal.

Mobs include: 

- The Death Of Five Alps. A noxious dreadhound with crippling summons.
- Fire Down The River. A lantern light capable of centralizing intense heat in specific points.
- Wardens. Fabled guardians of the forest, only one person has seen one and made it out alive.
