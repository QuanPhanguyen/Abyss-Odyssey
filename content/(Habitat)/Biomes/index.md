---
title: Biomes
---
Biomes are pockets of space containing a centralized theme and ecosystem. They are the nodes between paths, the rooms besides the hallways, and the plots behind book covers. They are new worlds containing concepts each different than the last. 

Biomes range in danger, size, and appearance. There is an utterly mundane acre of purple wheat on sun-baked hills where no fauna exist, or a room no larger than a closet where you speak into a terminal, or a hellscape swallowed by flame and overrun with demons.
