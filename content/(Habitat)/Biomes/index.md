title: Biomes
---
Biomes are pockets of space containing a centralized theme and ecosystem. They are the nodes between paths, the rooms besides the hallways, and the plots behind book covers. They are new worlds containing concepts each different than the last. 

Biomes range in danger, size, and appearance. There is an utterly mundane acre of purple wheat on sun-baked hills where no fauna exist, or a room no larger than a closet where you speak into a terminal, or a hellscape swallowed by flame and overrun with demons.

Creating a map of biomes requires thinking on a different plane from mapping out a regular location. Depending on a myriad of known and unknown factors, the very same path that would lead you to a garden of roses may instead take you to a massif thousands of feet in the sky. The gateways might not even connect the biomes in the same location. You may enter a biome through a gateway and find yourself deep underwater with the gateway behind you long gone.

The reward for navigating these perilous pathways is simple: otherworldly materials and concepts. Here is a compendium of biomes Giskenroc has charted out throughout its history.

Tag Notes:

The Entering Conditions tag stands for whether Giskenroc understands how to enter this biome or whether they stumble upon it by chance. There will be a percentage indicating how often they are able to intentionally enter it.

Danger levels for biomes fall under the following categories:
1. Safe - Completely harmless biome with no threats. Recommended for everyone.
2. Mildly Dangerous - A biome similar to Giskenroc with natural fauna/flora or structures which could be life threatening under the certain conditions. Recommended to new adventurers.
3. Moderately Dangerous - A biome with considerable danger. Those who enter are at considerable risk of losing their lives without prior knowledge or considerable experience. Recommended for most adventurers.
4. Extremely Dangerous - Biomes which are typically never entered. These are reserved for the most sturdy veterans of their craft, and they must obtain a special permit from the council of Trebytheus in order to begin an expedition. Not recommended for anyone at all.
5. Unknown - A danger level reserved for biomes which have zero survivors. These biomes have a special naming system to illustrate their entering conditions.
