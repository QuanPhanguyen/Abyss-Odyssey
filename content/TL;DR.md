This campaign takes place in a world colloquially referred to as the Habitat. It contains an innumerable amount of terrariums within, each enclosed and dubbed "Biomes." Biomes are pocket worlds 

Our story takes place within one of these Biomes, where a barren, grim, dark landscape of blackstone and cobble named Giskenroc shoddily stands.
