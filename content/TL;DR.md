title: TL;DR
---
This campaign takes place in a world colloquially referred to as the Habitat. It contains an innumerable amount of terrariums within, each enclosed and dubbed "Biomes." Biomes are pocket worlds with their own ecosystems.

Our story takes place within one of these Biomes, where a barren, grim, dark landscape of blackstone and cobble named Giskenroc shoddily stands. It is a kingdom whose technology has surpassed all other societal factors, a fact easily surveyable at a glance with how careening and violent it is.  

Life within Giskenroc is perilous for those who wish to live even a remotely modest lifestyle. Citizens can survive off of menial tasks and everyday handiwork, but in order to obtain necessities like shelter or provide for their families, they must be prepared to put themselves in danger. They can aid in dubious experiments, work in unsafe factories, or put everything on the line to explore other biomes. 

Three major factions drive the empire's direction as of this moment. The Congregant of Aerovar, a church focused on the sun, the Giskenroc Dynasty, the royal line who founded the kingdom, and the Kyezenthue, a coalition of researchers and blacksmiths dedicated to ancient technology.
