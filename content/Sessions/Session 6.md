5/20/502 LM

We open with the same party we had in [[Session 4]].  [[Seraphina (Biome Researcher)|Seraphina]] and [[Culain (Engineer)|Culain]] are now scientific micro-celebrities, although their research and persons are completely owned by the church. Their research is slow and steady, but they've hit another roadblock. They learn that an off-site church outside of the capital has what they need. Coincidentally, this church hasn't responded to any official inquiries in some time. A taskforce is requested and granted, comprising of Seraphina, Culain, [[Violet (Old Timer)|Violet]], [[Four (Cult Leader)|Four]], and [[Relic (Minor Cult Member)|Relic]]. An overseer will come along to make sure nothing goes wrong.

Before entering the main church, a large demonstration can be seen outside. They're rallying in the push between loose expedition rules and strict safety guidelines. The horde begins to mob the group upon noticing Seraphina. They accost her for their tabloids, hoping to get something out of her. Seraphine lets out a Freudian slip about wishing to go out and explore biomes herself, which becomes fuel for the looser expedition rules faction. 

Upon being guided to the back of the church by a group of masked henchmen, the two of them meet Lucille and Lucantor once again. They reveal themselves to be the babysitters for this trip.  Lucille says that only one of them will be needed, and allows the group to pick which of them they want to join the party. After some deliberation, they choose Lucantor. Lucille celebrates and leaves.

Lucantor takes them out in public after disguising herself. They take a public ride share to the outskirts. By the time they make it to their destination, there's only one other person with them. They exit and head to the church. The man follows. Relic attempts to strike up a conversation with the stranger, who reveals them to be an eccentric investigator by the name of [[Rue (Conspiracy Theorist)]]. Lucantor does not care and enters the bleak church.

The church is littered with corpses. Fresh, from a few days ago. Lucantor tells everyone to split up, presumably to speed things up. She heads to the second floor and Seraphina follows. The others investigate the rooms on the first floor. Violet finds that the podium in the center of the church has a slab with three empty holes. Rue finds a blue orb in one of the rooms and gets ambushed by four zombies. They immediately knock him unconscious. In a quick scuffle, they are dealt with and Rue is resuscitated to four hit points. The blue orb fits nicely into the slate in the podium.

Meanwhile, Seraphina attempts idle chatter with Lucantor, who has no interest in their conversation. They look through the second floor while ignoring what's happening beneath them. Lucantor loots a room, finding a book and a cloak of protection, which she gives to Seraphina. Seraphina finds a beautiful banquet hall with delicious gourmet foods scattered throughout. She fails a wisdom saving throw and finds the food to be delectable, although doesn't eat any. Lucantor heads to the other room, which is a storage containing a hatch to the roof.

On the bottom floor, Violet and Rue continue investigating areas in this church. They find a secret entrance behind a painting leading underground. 

Seraphina, Relic, Four, and Lucantor head to the roof. Relic slips off and breaks her neck, going unconscious. As everyone panics, Lucantor jumps down and stabilizes her, dumping her off at the entrance. Seraphina and Four find another blue orb. They slot it into the podium.

Everyone heads down into the underground. They discover two more rooms. One seems to be an armory with two longswords and a chestplate. The other seems to be some vile, nefarious lich room with essences being extracted from a heap of corpses. This room is ignored. The other is looted, with Violet and Four being unknowingly cursed. They enter the final room, where a blue orb rests on a pedestal. As the others examine the room, Relic inspects the pedestal. 

A sketchy, black hand shoots out of the orb and grabs Relic by the neck. It goes ethereal and brings Relic and it up to the first floor. Relic takes one attack and immediately dies. 

Everyone makes their way up. Lucantor is able to keep the spirit suppressed with stunning blows while the party gangs up on it. The fight is relatively easy with Lucantor. Once it fades away, the blue orb drops from it. They slot it into the third hole, and the podium slides open to reveal a staircase heading down. Four mourns her friend Relic while the others head down.

This room is a miniature vault, containing scrolls, books, and documents. An unusual augment is mixed in with everything. Seraphina and Culain are able to find the document they need. Lucantor takes the augment and heads up. She gives it to Four and tells her to use it. The session ends as Four carries Relics body out of the church.
