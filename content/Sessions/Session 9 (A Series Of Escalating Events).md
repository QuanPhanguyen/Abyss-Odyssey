*Fir beings to get accustomed to their new life.*
<br>
6/12/502 LM

The session opens alone with Fir. A brief recap informs them that in the two weeks between the last session and this one, they’ve spent most of their time moving in and getting accustomed to their new environment. Nabon has been rather reserved about this sudden change, allowing Fir to get used to things. The scene pans into his office, where the two of them are having a discussion. Fir is informed that their organization’s objective is to uncover the hidden history of the kingdom. Although Nabon is much more informed about this, being part of the original council, an oath had been made between the original elves to never disclose this information. Fir was a loophole to circumvent this vow. He hands Fir several stacks of paper and tells him to study. Nabon doesn’t want to reveal Fir before he is ready.

On the way home, Fir walks by a beggar and gives him some copper without his knowledge. They walk down a dark alleyway. A large longsword cleaves through the night, aiming for Fir’s neck. It is promptly parried by a low blade. As sparks illuminate the pathway, two figures are revealed. The aggressor is a gargantuan bugbear. Fir’s defendant is the beggar seen earlier. A minor scuffle breaks out before Fir shuts things down with a Charm Person. Twig comes along on their patrol upon hearing the commotion. 

The bugbear discloses his name as Log and says that Fir must be smited down for being an evil shapeshifter. Fir attempts to make things appear normal to little avail. Bobbits, the unseen bodyguard, remains quiet. Twig finds the situation too suspicious and decides to bring everyone to the local authority, which coincidentally is Nabon’s office. 

Nabon attempts to make sense of the random ensemble appearing at his door. He finds it concerning that Log knows Fir by name and asks everyone to come in. It is implied that Bobbits is a part of their organization, an agent in the shadows whose current objective is to protect Fir. Log says that he received an official invoice from the church to carry out this act. Nabon inspects it, declares it a forgery, and draws out a hidden curse. It was intended to eliminate Log after he fulfilled his task. Upon learning this, Log is placated, and the charm condition ends.

Nabon extrapolates information from what is going on. He infers that an antagonistic faction has discovered Fir’s revival and wishes to eliminate him while he is still considered missing. Nabon could reveal Fir’s existence to make future assassination attempts more difficult, but he thinks there is an opportunity in disguise here. He invokes the king’s name to hold authority over Twig’s actions. It is subtly revealed that Twig is an underling of the King. By staging Fir as bait, he can use Log, Bobbits, and Twig to catch the agents attempting to assassinate Fir. He decides to hold an event with a surprise announcement at the end. Hopefully, this will be enough to draw out those who know what the announcement is (the reveal of Fir) and spur them into action.

While the four think of how they’re going to set up. The spotlight shifts to Violet. We learn Violet has been doing relatively normal, but she feels uneasy from the large events occurring in Giskenroc, the disappearance of Seraphina being a key one. Her ghostly tendencies lead her to see an event posted on a board. As an invite-only exclusive, Violet speaks to Nabon to get in. Nabon recognizes her from the Seraphina experiments, which Violet dislikes greatly as she is trying to keep a low profile, and gladly lets her in. Violet does not like all of the attention she is receiving.

The four create their plan, and the night of the gathering comes. Violet arrives in a fancy dress and tries to skulk around stealthily. The ghosts are leading her upstairs, which is where everyone else is lying in wait, hoping to catch the assassins. The moment she sneaks above, she establishes eye contact with Twig. A magical darkness immediately envelops the hallway and the room Fir is being kept in. A turn goes by while the players figure out what’s going on. Out of the darkness, four strikes appear. Fir is immediately knocked, and the focus is on keeping them alive. Through a tense conflict, Fir survives the assassination attempt, and the group is able to capture two assassins. Downstairs, Fir is announced to be alive. Upstairs, the two assassins explode and dispense the same purplish curse. The session ends.

Everyone levels up.
