*Violence is the answer.*

10/3/502 LM

The session opens on a new character wading through a street parade. It’s a citywide funeral for the death of Bishop Four, lasting until the sun comes out. The new character is described to be a fashionable woman with purple locks and a unicorn’s horn. A commotion arises in the distance. She heads over, seemingly knowing something. 

We cut to the beginning of the day, where she’s getting ready to open up her boutique. As she flips the sign from closed to open, we see [[Bobbits (Beggar)|Bobbits]] waiting outside. They begin a conversation, where we learn the name of this unicorn: [[Gemma (Boutique Owner)|Gemmy]]. Bobbits asks for a favor. He’s following up on the lead he obtained in Session 12. There’s an organization in Gemmy’s neighborhood, the Hands of Aggavar, and Bobbits would like to schedule a meeting with them. As Gemmy lives within the shady District 13, she has close ties with the nefarious business conducted here even if she (supposedly) isn’t nefarious herself. 

Gemmy leads Bobbits to the establishment where they reside. The leader of the group, Mac, holds a table for him and they begin to talk business. As Bobbits attempts to gather the information he wants, [[An Ny (Kid)|An Ny]] walks into the establishment, led by another member of the Hands. Although Bobbits notices her, she does not notice him, and she quietly enters an adjacent room. An Ny proceeds to have an interview with the Hands, presumably trying to join their ranks for income. 

Bobbit’s negotiations go south, and the room quickly turns hostile toward him even with Gemmy’s help. Seeing as talks are going nowhere, Bobbit’s decides to take the preemptive move and begin the brawl. In a mighty round, he’s able to knock out two of the assassins beside him while taking an aggressive stance against Mac. He’s able to eventually hold Mac hostage against the remaining members, demanding information for Mac’s life. At this point, things quickly begin spiraling out of control. An Ny hears the commotion from the room and becomes possessed by BUDDY. She goes on a violent murdering spree, killing over half of the members by her lonesome. Gemmy finds things ramping up beyond her capabilities, and uses one of her abilities to teleport home. 

Meanwhile, because of this unexpected third party, Mac and Bobbits are forced to work together. They are eventually able to put a stop to An Ny’s reign of terror, with Bobbit’s pretending to kill her. With the organization left in shambles and most members dead, Mac agrees to give Bobbit’s the information. He takes him to the main office upstairs where Bobbits is able to collect a myriad of documents. There are other treasures within the office vault, but Bobbits opts not to take them. Mac wishes him good luck, as the information contained is dangerous to both of their lives, and leaves with the rest of the Hands. Moments later, the building is set ablaze.

In the aftermath, we look at our players and see where they’re at. An Ny wakes up in the dumpster, unsure of what happened, and is devastated to learn her employment site has burned down. She goes to return home, cutting through the parade. Bobbits heads to the boutique to check up on Gemmy, cutting through the parade to do so. Gemmy closes up shop early today after what’s happened, and decides to go home. She cuts through the parade.

As all of them end up in the parade at the same time, a commotion breaks out. The three of them come from different directions and head towards it. There are funeral crashers. [[Seraphina (Biome Researcher)|Seraphina]] and her expeditioning party have returned.
