3/29/502 LM

At the same day as [[Session 2]], [[Gogoat (Merchant)|Gogoat]] entertains a late night visitor who sells him an egg from deep in Dezerekt. He sends this egg away to a friend of his, [[Lily Haxley]], who specializes in this type of research.

A month later, [[Gogoat (Merchant)|Gogoat]] and [[Bjorn (Amnesiac)|Bjorn]] head over to the laboratory to discover the results of their egg investigation. Upon arriving there, they find a dead assistant researcher, the egg hatched, and the place a mess. [[Twig (Government Robot)|Twig]] joins the party as [[Lily Haxley]] screams. 

Upon investigation, it is found that the creature emits a dark, viscous slime, entering a drain pipe. The cage seemed purposefully unlocked, the researcher has their eyes missing, and the researcher died from blunt force trauma to the back of their head.

The group head into the sewers hoping to catch up with the creature. They become startled by [[Violet (Old Timer)|Violet]], who apparently spends a lot of time down here. The four of the follow the trail left behind by the creature. [[Violet (Old Timer)|Violet]] shares knowledge that this creature is likely extremely dangerous.

They run into a pack of giant rats, fleeing from something, and enter a fight. The rats explode when killed due to their mechanical diet. As the fight conclude, [[Violet (Old Timer)|Violet]] notices something in the corner of their eye. A presence. They understand that it is something that should never be comprehended, and that merely looking at it will cause an incredibly dicey roll to occur.

With this newly obtained information, the gang decide to leave, thinking the scope of this issue is beyond them. They find the nearest exit, a hatch to a main street, and try to leave. But the creature has left a trap, and snares [[Violet (Old Timer)|Violet]] to the opposite wall. 

At the same time, a gargantuan eel swimming through the sewage comes up behind them. It is badly injured, presumably by the same creature. [[Gogoat (Merchant)|Gogoat]] becomes entangled and grappled by the creature while the others try to break the hardened slime covering the exit. In a narrow victory, the eel is defeated.

The group refocus their efforts on leaving, but become too hasty. They don't take necessary precaution, and once again, the creature sneaks up on them. [[Gogoat (Merchant)|Gogoat]] is the victim once again. However, [[Gogoat (Merchant)|Gogoat]] rolls a Natural 20, and succeeds on the wisdom saving throw. He experiences eldritch knowledge beyond the scope any mortal should be able to withstand, and goes unconscious. He levels up. With this momentum, the group is able to leave and get out of there, with [[Twig (Government Robot)|Twig]] rolling a Natural 20 as well to succeed on the grapple check against the creature.

Afterward, [[Gogoat (Merchant)|Gogoat]] is dropped off at a local facility while [[Twig (Government Robot)|Twig]] leaves to find a local official to warn. She encounters a strange man, an official unlike anyone she's ever seen. In a turn of events undisclosed to everyone other than [[Twig (Government Robot)|Twig]], she returns to the group to let everyone know officials have been notified. [[Violet (Old Timer)|Violet]] disappears.

[[Bjorn (Amnesiac)|Bjorn]] levels up.
[[Twig (Government Robot)|Twig]] levels up.
