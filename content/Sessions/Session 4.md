4/19/502 LM

Today is the anniversary of Prince Desolpe's crowning. Citizens flock to the capital streets, enjoying each other's company and night life. [[Violet (Old Timer)|Violet]] is plagued by the resurgence of ghosts in her life. It is revealed that as Violet returns to her former strength, otherworldly force are beginning to haunt her again. They take her to significant events where she is needed. 

Violet searches the streets for her final victim. She keeps company with an elderly old woman for the holiday night, and then disposes of her, silently and painlessly. She reaches her former level. From her vantage point on a high hill, she notices one of the research spires crawling with spirits, hanging off the sides in masse like ants clinging onto a log. They stare at her from miles out, beckoning her to come.

Meanwhile, [[Seraphina (Biome Researcher)|Seraphina]] and [[Culain (Engineer)|Culain]] are attempting and failing another experiment within the same spire. [[Four (Cult Leader)|Four]] and [[Relic (Minor Cult Member)|Relic]], the former a friend of Seraphina, knock at the door. The four of them greet each other at the doorway. Unbeknownst to the two gnomes, Violet had been quietly behind them the entire time they walked over. She sees a coalition of ghosts keenly crowding around Seraphina, a sign that something monumental is about to occur. 

Seeing a strange crowd forming, Seraphina senses an opportunity. The two lab researchers have been working on a theory. They hypothesize that sunlight is diminishing at a miniscule but exponential amount over time. However, they require data to prove it. Data from the period of history before the 67th year of Giskenroc's history, which is heavily restricted knowledge.

There are two locations which could satisfy this historical criteria. They could venture to the kingdom on the second floor of the capital and stealth into the vaults with the help of Seraphina's dad. Or they could enter the [[Giskenroc/Locations/index|Aerovian Church]] and sneak into the archives with the help of Four and Relic's bishop leader. After weighing the pros and cons, they decide to head to the kingdom's vaults. 

The party heads up to the second floor and heads to Seraphina's home. They fail to convince her father to help. He warns them about the consequences of getting caught. Seraphina says she'll go to the church otherwise. She's going to do this one way or another. He prefers they try their luck there, and casts Aid on the party after a heartwarming conversation between the two of them.

Heading back down to Four's contact, we learn she is in a cult named The Community. Her leader, [[Helion Maximinimus]], is a suspicious individual, although his motives are never addressed. He agrees to help the five enter the archives on the condition they tell him what they are entering for. Four and Relic attempt to lie, but their augment forces them to tell the truth. Violet gleams he has an ulterior motive however doesn't push it.

They enter the Aerovian Church and sneak into the backrooms. Upon reaching the archive door, Four presses their hand against the lock. This immediately triggers an alarm. The five of them try to deal with the situation to little avail. A blond haired human startles them by asking what they're doing. They attempt to make excuses, but she figures things out by looking at the melted lock on the floor, a byproduct of Culain's lockpicking success. Four and Relic succeed on a Religion check to recognize the teenager as [[Saintess Lucille du Promade III]], a very prominent figure within the church.

A frightening figure, seemingly heightening gravity on everyone with just his speech, begins to approach from around the corner. Lucille opens the door with a wave of her hand and whispers "Good Luck!" to the party. They hear the male voice say "I told you it's dangerous, there's-" before the entrance shuts close. 

They enter an archive, like a library. Relic's Detect Magic senses Abjuration magic on nearly every item, other than the chandelier and marble statues. They have Transfiguration magic. Seraphina and Culain find the book the need. They decide to destroy the transfigured item at the same time they pick up the book. The objects begin to morph into creatures as the book is picked up, but well placed attacks from Violet, Relic, and Culain destroy them before they can fully form. 

They leave the archive stealthily, however it looks like no one is there anymore. They return to where Helion left them and see him talking to Lucille. Helion attempts to blackmail Lucille as Relic succeeds on an insight check on Lucille as well. Before he can get to any terms, Lucille beheads him with her hand. 

It turns out this blond girl is [[Saintess Lucantor du Promade III]], Lucille's twin sister. She gives Four his insignia, calling her the bishop now. She tells them to clean the body up, but it is revealed she was speaking to her posse as a crowd of masked priests come out of hidden nooks and crannies to remove all stains of ungodliness. The sessions ends abruptly here.

Culain levels up.
Four levels up.
