4/26/502 LM

Roll initiative. This session opens mid-combat. [[Violet (Old Timer)|Violet]], [[Twig (Government Robot)|Twig]], [[Gogoat (Merchant)|Gogoat]], [[Aria (Hired Mercenary)|Aria]], are in the middle of a combat with a sentient tree monster and its two smaller branch children. A bountiful spring with lush trees and crystalline water is the battlefield. [[Bjorn (Amnesiac)|Bjorn]] lies on the side, unconscious, with a gaping hole in his chest.

As the turn order goes down, more is revealed about how everyone ended up in this situation. Ever since [[Session 3]], Gogoat has been hearing and seeing things in his day to day life. He tried to brush it off, but the panging is growing increasingly intense. He decides to take a trip into Dezerekt to search for answers. 

The original party who went into the sewers decide to go with him. Aria is a hired hand for extra protection. Dezerekt is a biome with danger increasing with depth. Gogoat's visions take him to a dead end on the first floor. He feels compelled to whistle in a certain manner, and a doorway opens up to place the party in the situation they are presently in. 

The fight is long and grueling due to unfortunate luck from certain members. Multiple characters go down multiple times. Gogoat and Twig do their best to keep everyone up, including mending the grievous wound on Bjorn. However, strange things begin to occur to Gogoat throughout the fight. Some spells feel as though there is a second caster speaking underneath his vocal layer. His bardic inspiration materializes symbols of gold halos and red afterimages. 

Once the fight ends, the group discovers the door behind them to be shut tight. Gogoat feels an urge to continue down this secret passageway. There are two paths, left and right. A closed door lies beyond the spring. On the left is a dark beach with an ocean expanse further than the eye can see. On the right is a deep, tropical jungle, also never-ending. After taking watch and long resting, the party decide to go left, as Aria heard sounds of human humming during her shift.

The beach has no tracks, and a single rowboat large enough to fit six. Gogoat intrinsically knows where to go. They depart, with Twig turning into a Giant Frog attached on a rope to scan underneath the waters. After some hours, Twig notices a behemoth leviathan approaching. They inform the party. They attempt to out row it, but fail. The creature crashes into the vessel and dismantles it, sending splinters of boat work everywhere. The party latch onto pieces of driftwood and survive for 18 hours, suffering levels of exhaustion, before making it to shore.

They take another long rest while keeping a close watch on the single gateway ahead. Aria notices the white slime from Session 3, but dismisses it as she was not there, simply considering it a part of the terrain. With the long rest completed, Gogoat decides to take a look. He fails a wisdom saving throw and becomes entranced, going past this area without telling any of the others. Twig is close by, however everyone else is not.

This chamber is composed of marble tiling. It is a pure space devoid of any existence. Twig looks to see what has caught Gogoat's mind, and sees a single carved figure at the end of the hall. It rapidly morphs, as the same white substance begins filing out between the lines of the tile to form a ball of transmuted flesh, the figure now snapped in half and hung upside down to create a face. 

Twig tries to pry Gogoat away, but the slime rapidly moves around him like a protective cocoon. It does not allow anything to interfere with him. The rest of the group arrive at this point. The figure makes a devastating group attack, crawling forward at unnatural speeds and flinging it's excess flesh over the party. Three of them fail their checks, and are encased in a hard shell they must break free of. In the meanwhile, Gogoat is trudging along to the edge of the room, where an abyss is seen. It is a deadly pitfall with almost no chance of survival. 

The party infers that killing the creature will destroy the protective casing around Gogoat. Bjorn sacrifices all memory of Gogoat from the past two months to amplify his augment, giving him a powerful bonus for the fight. Gogoat senses something amiss, but doesn't have the capacity to break free from the hold. In the end, the party is too little too late, as Gogoat takes his final step over the edge and disappears from view. At the same time, the creature dissolves into slime and seeps into the lining of the floor once again. 

The session ends.

They do not respond in the Gisken tongue.
