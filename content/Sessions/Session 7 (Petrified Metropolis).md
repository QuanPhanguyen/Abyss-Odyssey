*Seraphina fulfills her dream.*
<br>

6/3/502 - 6/10/502 LM

Open on a one-on-one session with [[Seraphina (Biome Researcher)|Seraphina]]. [[Saintess Lucantor du Promade III]] and [[Saintess Lucille du Promade III]] knock at her lab in the church. They inform her that her father has come to speak with the [[Current Cardinal|Aero]]. Seraphina is confused. The twins help Seraphina sneak into a loft above the hall of the Aero to listen in on their conversation. Seraphina's father Sylvaar is upset at the thought of his daughter being used for a publicity stunt. Aero Rh'aap says it is out of his hands. They continue a vague conversation about the Three's Council. It appears they know each other personally. Rh'aap offers to leave the decision up to Seraphina, which Sylvaar scoffs at. He leaves after venting his concerns.

The Aero calls Seraphina down from her hidden spot on the high beams. He informs her that it is a chaotic and turbulent time in Giskenroc and, unfortunately, she is caught in the midst of it. Because of her comments on legislation, she must now venture out of Giskenroc to put her money where her mouth is and aid the church's position. He hopes she will be able to get something out of her time in the spotlight. Seraphina chases her father home and finds him missing. There is a note telling her he will be gone for a week to complete some unresolved matters. Seraphina's expedition sets out in the same time.

*Some Time Passes Until The Real Session.*
<br>

Seraphina sits in a carriage protected by four elite Aerovian guards. Inside are two more biome veterans, [[Bjorn (Amnesiac)|Bjorn]] and [[Bar (Bear)|Bar the Bare]]. [[Rue (Conspiracy Theorist)|Rue]] also requested to join this venture, which Seraphina had to reason to say no to. They've arrived at the biome connector early, and are waiting for the right time to enter their peaceful biome. While having a conversation, an unnatural silence washes over them like a spell. They can make no noise. Seraphina and Bar notice something afoot, and open the cart tarp to reveal a hooded figure with a magical crown above their head. They've already killed one guard without the party noticing. 

It's apparent that this foe is beyond the scope of their abilities. The guards signal for them to leave. They ride into the connector while watching the rest of the guards quickly falling. Seraphina jots down an accurate depiction of the assassin. 

They are the first to enter the [[Petrified Metropolis]]. It is a sprawling urban architectural maze, a gray-scale color palette with a constant, bright white light filling the sky with no origin. There's a seemingly infinite number of skyscrapers with walkways connecting multiple buildings, on multiple levels, to create a 3D visual maze. Landmarks appear every so often as spiral towers with pointed tips, protruding higher than the skyscrapers besides them. 

The party heads to the roof of their skyscraper to survey. They decide to head to the nearest spiral tower. They leave the carriage behind and take the horses, only packing the essentials. Rations are the key resource to not run out of. As they head over, a colossal figure fires a burst of energy to eradicate the walkway they're on. The party is split as the walkway begins careening down. Bar and Seraphina safely make it to one end. Bjorn catches Rue who makes a desperate leap as the ground gives way below his feet. The horses careen over and hit multiple walkways with sickening thuds before the noises end due to how far they are falling.

They become lost from each other. Bar and Seraphina reach the spiral tower first and discover a magical tablet in the top room. They decide to wait for Bjorn and Rue. When Rue arrives he fiddles with the contraption and realizes it is a communication device. Whatever they write on here is delivered to an unknown party. This is historic news for Seraphina, who knows that Giskenroc has never established contact with a foreign civilization. 

Their conversation leaves more questions than answers as they both try to figure out who the other is. The foreign party expects the party to be able to defeat the colossal figure with relative ease, considering where they are from. Ultimately, the foreign party tells them to mark their tower and look for the exit. They will do the same. The tablet runs out of energy and dispenses a used canister. 

The party heads out to the next tower. On the way, Rue spots a fresh canister on the ground level due to his augment, a flying cube. Seraphina attempts to gather it and gets violently impaled with a tungsten rod from a sentry. After length deliberation, the party decides messing with the sentries is not worth it. They continue north. 

Upon reaching the tower, the examine the highest room and find no tablet. Rue discovers a hatch at the base of a nearby building. They drop down and find themselves in a hallway of white padded walls. A canister lies at the end. They move for it and are jumped by mechanical arachnids ripping through the walls. Bar the Bare saves the party from demise. They retrieve the canister. 

They decide to continue up. Upon making it to their third tower, there is still not a tablet at the roof of this room either. Rue suggests heading back to use the canister. They agree, taking a slight detour to examine one more adjacent tower on the way back. This tower contains nothing as well. 

They relaunch communications with the foreign party. The foreign party tells them the open exit has been found, and they give them the location. The foreign party finds it odd that they should be in the same spot right now. The party heads to the exit. It is another hatch with a similar hallway. The party investigates the walls preemptively, but find deep tunnels with no signs of movement. At the end of the hallway is a closed stone door and a terminal. The terminal has catalogued every message sent in this space, including conversations the party did not partake in. Seraphina begins to record them down.

The moment she begins interacting with the terminal, a sweeping tidal wave of mechanical arachnids begin careening down toward them. Bjorn and Rue buy time by firing ranged spells, while Bar opens the door. Seraphina is able to mark down 66% of the logs before everyone decides to leave. They shut the door on the scuttling creatures.

A hooded figure is waiting for them when they return. It is a nearby citizen. Upon seeing Seraphina, they exclaim in wonder, asking where she has been. Seraphina tentatively asks what they mean. It is revealed they have been gone for five months.
