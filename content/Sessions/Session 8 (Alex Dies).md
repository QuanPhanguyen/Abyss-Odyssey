6/8/502 LM

The session opens with a flashback. [[Four (Cult Leader)|Four]] is remembering her early days at the Community. [[Giskenroc/Factions/Congregant of Aerovar (Light)/Notable Figures/Helion Maximinimus|Helion]] enters the center area to introduce [[Relic (Minor Cult Member)|Relic]] to the cult. The two of them immediately hit it off, proclaiming to be best friends forever. Out of the blue, while continuing their conversation, Relic asks Four why she allowed her to die. Four panics and makes excuses, insisting it wasn’t her fault, as Relic continues to berate her. Four wakes up from her nightmare. 

Her throat is parched, her head is spinning, and she doesn’t know what time it is. At her bedside table is the augment from [[Session 6 (Four)]]. She has not attuned to it because she feels that doing so will set Relic’s death in stone. A knock at the door captures her attention.

Disheveled, Four walks to the door. She’s hesitant about answering. However, something captures her attention. The mysterious visitor has a voice exactly like Relic's. Four opens the door to see [[Alex (Bumpkin)|Alex]]. Alex is on a mission to find the families of all the deceased people from Session 2 for atonement. She mistakenly thinks that the Community holds some clues. Four initially wishes to wave Alex off, but feels a strange sense of kinship from this person who has the same mannerisms as her dead friend. The two of them set off to the guild in search of information.

At the guild, Alex and Four easily obtain information about the next of kin for those on the infamous Icynia’s Plain’s expedition. However, both [[Hesinx (Blind Aristocrat)|Hesinx]] and “Doe” used fake names when signing up on this expedition, making their identity difficult to gather. [[Bjorn (Amnesiac)|Bjorn]] makes a surprise appearance and greets Alex. He gives them a tip on Hesinx, saying they may be the famous daughter of the Wiel family. Four continues to find Alex more and more similar to Relic, but she can’t figure out why. Four and Alex set out with this new found information.

[[Clobo the Globo (Clown Goblin)|Clobo]] is the first on the list. They realize he used a fake location and that he has no next of kin to alert. They move onto Azazz. They discover his home life is complicated, and neither of his parents particularly care about his passing. His parents didn’t expect anything of him anyway.

Discouraged by this day not turning out how Alex envisioned it, the two of them set out to look for Hesinx Wiel. They find the Wiel manor quickly, and are greeted by Cassandra Wiel, the mother of the household. Before Alex can drop the news of Hesinx’s death, they are quickly ushered into the house at the mere mention of having news. Edmond Wiel, the father, joins them at the dinner table. Alex tells them the story of what happened. The parents go silent. Edmond asks to be excused and heads upstairs. A few minutes of awkward silence go by before he returns with another elf. He seems to be Hesinx’s older brother. Alex tells the story again.

Shen, Hesinx’ brother, asks how much his parents paid to get them to say this. The two are confused as both Cassandra and Edmond console him. Alex makes an insight check to discern neither parent cares about Hesinx that much, or at least not as much as they do Shen, and they wish for him to move on already instead of wasting his time looking for her. Shen bangs the table to silence everyone before asking his parents to leave so he can speak to the two guests privately. He “asks” Four and Alex to take a walk with him. 

They go to a nearby pastry shop. He informs them it is a spot the two siblings liked to frequent. He asks Alex to tell him the story again, but to tell the full story, starting from the very beginning when Alex met Hesinx. Alex does and he is grateful. He tells them they have his favor, and he hopes for them to attend her funeral. Unfortunately, he has some pressing matters to attend to and exits while leaving a hefty payment for the two to treat themselves to. They do not.

As the two of them get ready to leave, the shockwave of a huge explosion knocks them off their feet. Shellshocked, battered, and confused, the two of them look at a horrific scene of heavily injured citizens who they can’t even tell are alive or grievously wounded. They have pounding traumatic flashbacks of death. The two of them do what they can to save as many people as they can. Alex uses bardic inspiration on Four. Her bardic inspiration has turned from a happy go lucky merry tune to a low and steadfast hum. For the dozen that they are able to save, there is another dozen who unfortunately pass.

Officials arrive on scene shortly after. They patch everyone up to the best of their abilities. A head captain of this brigade gives Alex a strange look when noting her voice. While the victims of this accident are undergoing check-ups, the captain specifically picks out Alex and Four. He invites them to his office nearby for further examination. Four succeeds on a history check to discern that this is a highly ranked elf, one on the council of Trebytheus, which answers directly to the king.

Upon entering his local quarters, he takes them upstairs to his domain. The door locks behind them. He introduces himself as [[Nabon Trebytheus]] and begins to interrogate them. Alex tries to ask if he knows about “Doe,” but the man beats her to the punch. It seems he knows a lot. He knows who Alex is, knows about the expedition she went on, and comes to the conclusion that Fir Trebytheus, scion of the Council of Trebytheus, went with her on this ill-fated excursion. Four gathers that Nabon also finds something strange about Alex’s voice. He then tells Alex she is going to die here.

Nabon gives her a decision, both of which will eliminate the identity that is Alex in some way. The first is that he wipes her memories of Doe, and Alex will go back to being the joyful air genasi she always used to be. This option is unappealing to Alex because it means she will lose the today's memories and will inevitably miss Hesinx’s funeral. The second choice is to take on the responsibilities of Fir. She will spend her life impersonating him until his insurmountable task is complete. Alex as everyone knows her will have died in the explosion, and her life will end.

Still reeling in the aftermath of her trauma, Alex agrees without asking for any further clarification. With a vow placed on the three of them using Nabon’s augment, the three of them are sworn to secrecy. From this moment on, Alex is no longer, and Fir is reborn.
