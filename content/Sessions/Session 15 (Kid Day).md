*Child labor is legal in Giskenroc.*

2/21/49X LM

The session opens with seven kids in an elevator going down a mineshaft. They’re crouched and huddled up in the dark. A flashback to earlier in the day explains what they’re doing here. At An Ny’s orphanage, their caretaker Mama has chosen them out for an expedition. Although the environment is poor, she herself flaunts a wealthy appearance and is using the children for labor. Through roll call, we learn them to be [[Kat (Kid 1)|Kat]], [[Tavian (Kid 2)|Tavian]], [[Plop (Kid 3)|Plop]], [[Milly (Kid 4)|Milly]], [[Worf (Dog)|Worf]], and [[Felicity (Kid 5)|Felicity]]. [[An Ny (Kid)|An Ny]] seems to be the youngest. They must venture underground into the mines and collect rare flowers used for healing potions. They may not come back until they each have at least one. They are given hard hats, a respirator, and a map drawn by the previous batch of kids who were sent down.

As the doors open, they’re greeted with a mossy underground. A low level of spores floats gently above the ground, faintly emitting light. Skeletal remains with respirators are spread all throughout. First on the map is a room with a swamp-like tree. Its branches slowly inch toward the characters, but at a leisurely pace. Hidden inside its trunk are two flowers. They experiment and discover that the gateway to the flowers opens slowly as the branches get close to them. Tavian decides to take the leap of faith and allow the branch to slither on him while Kat tries to pry the flower. Although the vines begin to invade Tavian’s orifices dangerously, reaching his skull and throat, Kat is able to nimbly sneak the flower without any harm befalling the young boy. They successfully obtain both flowers.

In the meanwhile, Worf, An Ny, and Plop wait near the entrance. A large, monstrous creature sneaks up behind them and kills Plop with a single strike of its spear. It then bumbles around and heads down the hallway, ignoring the others. An Ny and Worf follow it while hiding. They can see it’s a horrifying visage of a human, with an inflated head, pudgy, thick hands, and rows of sharp teeth. They find its hideout at the end of the hallway. They can see more wooden pikes lodged inside the wall, with human skeletons wrapped around them. The creature sticks Plop into the wall, grabs her small, delicate body with its giant-like hands, and tears her into two. It then grabs one of her organs specifically and begins eating it. 

Meanwhile, the others return to the entrance of their room and find the other kids missing. They dismiss this, thinking they’re dead. They move on to the water room. Within this room, there is a wall separating them from another patch of two flowers. The only way to get there is a water hole. Milly volunteers to dive in, since they can hold their breath for five minutes. The group ties a rope around him to pull him out in the case he gets stuck. They head in and navigate a maze-like waterway in the dark. They find a protrusion against one of the walls and nab it. They then decide to greed and try to explore more before getting stuck. Meanwhile, the entrance closes up with vines blocking the way. With the time it takes to clear the entrance and get Milly unstuck, they pull him out dead to drowning. 

Something strange happens to both Milly and Plop. They begin reconstituting themselves and come back to life. However, they have some strange side effects attached to them. They’re unbelievably happy and giddy, and have the strong urge to take off their respirator and to consume spores. Worf and An Ny are able to conduct a rescue operation to retrieve Milly from the hideout. They additionally find two flowers hidden in the corner of the room. Milly discovers the protrusion they obtained was another flower, but also that there is no way of making it to the other side of this chamber. The seven of them regroup. At this point, they have five flowers.

They visit a long and skinny chamber with two stone pedestals on both sides. A flower is locked away on each end. They decide to press symbols on the pedestal at the same time with Plop in the middle of the room giving direction with their message spell. They incorrectly guess and walls begin closing around them. An Ny and Tavian are able to sprint out quickly, but the others are not so fortunate. They are ground into bone powder and guts. Once again, they are reconstituted with the same effect. Milly, however, with their two deaths, turns into a vegetable fiend who wishes nothing more than to dig themselves into the ground and become one with the earth. The group ties them up and drags them around. They attempt the puzzle one more time but fail again, and the pedestals are destroyed. 

They move into the final room where sacs filled with a greenish-yellow pus are scattered all throughout. Inside, at the base, are flowers, although they can’t tell without getting into the sacs if they are the flowers they are looking for. They cautiously spread into the chamber. Felicity and Tavian carefully scan the room for a large insect or something similar, and instead find the horrendous human now named Mr. Bad Bad behind them. Three of them explode the sacs in a rush. The acidic contents kill Tavian and Worf. They are fortunate enough to find two more flowers within the contents of the three sacs they popped. They retrieve them. Mr. Bad Bad kills Milly a third time, who has been left at the entrance tied down. Milly turns into vines and branches, and Mr. Bad Bad departs with them. Everyone else takes their leave, all terminally ill except for An Ny, who has not been killed once. The session ends.
