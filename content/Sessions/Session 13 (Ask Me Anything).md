*What would you give to know the truth?*

9/18/502 LM

[[Twig (Government Robot)|Twig]] walks into a cozy tavern on the outskirts of the [[(Habitat)/Giskenroc/Locations/index|Forestry's Glade]]. They enter a dark room upstairs, where a mysterious man lies on the bed staring up at the ceiling. It is revealed Twig's owner is [[King|King Desolpe]]. When Desolpe sits up to greet Twig, they notice an unsettling presence behind his cloak. The king notices this, and comments on how Twig has grown stronger. He expresses his wish that Twig not grow too powerful, for their own sake. On that strange note, he asks them to pick up a book, Polythesis, for him at the local museum. He tells Twig to leave at a specific time and to "not ask anything about him."

Twig makes it to the museum and speaks to a receptionist, [[Yuan (Overworked)|Yuan]]. Yuan takes them to the back archive and grants them the book they desire. As they walk out, Twig bumps into [[Four (Cult Leader)|Four]] and [[Rekk (Father)|Rekk]]. Literally. The book spills from their hands. After a brief pause of worry, nothing happens, and they breath a sigh of relief (metaphorically). At this moment, [[Violet (Old Timer)|Violet]] walks into the room. Immediately, a purple tether snakes out of the book and connects with Violet's chest. The room is fully enclosed into a psuedo-phantom zone with no exit or entrance, and a mysterious entity appears above them all.

The mysterious entity, named Poly, takes the appearance of a boy with a swirled, unrecognizable face, and a cone hat. They have a child-like persona, and seem eager to hear stories. They explain they woke up because of Violet's mysterious background, which it has not seen anything like before. Poly offers them this game: they may ask anything they wish, about any topic, and he will answer the question as long as they tell him a wonderful story. However, the more "dangerous" the question is, the more "dangerous" the story will be, and they will be forced to experience this story first hand. They must ask three questions before he lets them go.

Violet, unsure about what she's getting into, asks a question which has been plaguing her throughout her lifetime: Where does her ability to see ghosts come from? In exchange for this question, Poly takes everyone in this room to one of Violet's past lives, the one directly before this one, where she was a researcher named Lily. Lily "died" with the rest of her adventuring party out in a cavernous biome against a giant bat. Poly recreates the setting with everyone playing different members of her party. 

The group must play a fickle game of bartering with Poly while fighting this bat. Although this is a recreation of Violet's experience, the details are vague and murky, and they can fiddle with features if Poly agrees to it. The group attempts to garner as many advantages as they can, but they fall short in the end, as Yuan and Four are mercilessly killed. In Four's final moments, they think about [[Relic (Minor Cult Member)|Relic]], and experience a peaceful death. Violet is able to change the past and save the others, however.

As they get transported back to the museum, Poly grants Violet the answer to her question. One of the astronauts "cursed" her before they departed on their expedition. Although the details are left vague, Poly thinks this is enough for Violet to figure out on her own. 

Now understanding the severity of the situation they're in, the group hesitates about their next two questions. They must ask something that is helpful, but not too helpful. Something interesting, but not dangerous. They settle on an issue plaguing the nation: Seraphina's whereabouts. 

They are taken to the [[Petrified Metropolis]], in one of the towers used for communication. However, they are playing the roles of the people Seraphina and Co. talked to when they were searching for a way out. They look at each other an find a brigade of undead. They use this recreation of the history to ask Seraphina questions regarding her safety and how she ended up in this situation. At the end of their questioning, the titan roaming the metropolis finds them and fires a devastating breath attack. Rekk falls unconscious and begins falling from the tower, but they complete the scenario in time for him to be saved as they return to the museum. 

Twig asks a personal question about whether or not Alex truly died, which satisfies Poly despite it not being a particularly dangerous question. As Poly sets up the scenario, something unexpected occurs once Nabon appears and creates the oath between the involved participants. The recreation gives away as Poly becomes irritated, talking about "another one." They tell Twig about how Alex has returned as Fir and promptly leave, saying their mood is spoiled. The session ends as Poly returns to their book.
