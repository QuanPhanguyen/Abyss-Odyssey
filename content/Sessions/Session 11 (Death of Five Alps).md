*What does the average expedition look like?*

6/15/502 LM

A cart travels through [[Spirit's Nook]]. Inside are five new characters. [[Obsith (Tired)]], [[Tipi (Thief)]], [[Will (Occultist)]], [[Rekk (Father)]], and [[Lyonson (Narcissist)]]. They are a group of experienced adventurers who head out on expeditions to collect materials for profit. Several days have gone by idly. A nice stash of resources have been collected. One morning, as the party prepares to head onward, Obsith notices some strange marks on a nearby tree. There is a long, horizontal deep imprint at its stump, as though a large axe struck into it. As Obsith comes up to it, there is a black shadow which jumps at her. It forms into a horrifying hand combined from wolf claw and human fingers. Obsith recognizes the minion as a byproduct from a named beast of the land, The Death of Five Alps. At the same moment, Lyonson notices the beast itself lurking at a distance.

The party departs in a hurry. Their horses drag the cart at a rapid pace, as Death quickly follows. They're forced into uncharted territory, where the trees grow sideways and nothing lies below them except an endless abyss. Branches are their pathways. The Death slings hordes and hordes of minions onto the cart. The party has a difficult time fending off the swarming hands. Will is knocked unconscious and dangles over the open air, a rope on the cart attached to his ankle. He is quickly pulled back up. Tipi is knocked unconscious and dragged off the cart. The party has a difficult choice of leaving Tipi behind or running away from Death. They can't leave their comrade behind and the gap between Death and them narrows.

The fight becomes more and more difficult as more people get taken overboard and more hands come on. Lyonson is knocked overboard as Death catches up. The group has no choice but to see Lyonson be mauled alive by an endless swarm of hands. The misfortune doesn't end here either, as Death takes a giant bite out of the side of the cart, catching Will. He is immediately killed. 

The cart falls into a circular pool and the land before them refracts and flips to place them into an endless expanse of walkable water and sky. In the distance, there is a circular podium made of of ruin blocks. Taking the cart on causes it to descend, and the reach a temple filled with unknown history. On the walls are depictions of two genasi leaders taking a civilization of spirits to a new land. Notably, this population is attuned to augments. The remaining party members scavenge this abandoned throne room and pick up a metal orb. 

We abruptly transition to a week after [[Session 10 (The Curious Case Of Seraphina Mistrivvin)|Session 10]]. [[Fir (Scion)|Fir]] meets with [[Oswaldi Prinipep|Oswaldi]] to pick up their augment. It is revealed the orb gathered in today's session is the same orb explored in the previous session.
