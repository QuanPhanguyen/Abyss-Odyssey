[[Alex (Bumpkin)|Alex]] and her two friends [[Niminee (Alex's Friend)|Niminee]] and [[Twig (Government Robot)|Twig]] go on an errand to pick something up for Alex's mother.

They venture to the capital and enter the [[Giskenroc/Locations/index|Bazaar]] where they meet [[Bjorn (Amnesiac)|Bjorn]], [[Ricky (Rich)|Ricky]], and [[Gogoat (Merchant)|Gogoat]]. Due to a mix-up, the package has been mistakenly given to someone else. They go to the local tavern, where the transaction occurred, to gather information. 

[[Violet (Old Timer)|Violet]] overhears a conversation in the tavern and offers her aid. She says the mistaken person can be found at a Kyezenthue fight club, and offer to take them there.

The group enter the capital and head into a spiraling network of shoddy buildings. They find the fight club and are denied entry. [[Ratzi Inbesia]], the owner of the fight club, says he will allow entry for the entire group if two people partake in a fight. [[Niminee (Alex's Friend)|Niminee]] and [[Gogoat (Merchant)|Gogoat]] volunteer.

The group gathers information in the club as the two contestants narrowly win their fight. [[Niminee (Alex's Friend)|Niminee]] gets knocked out and becomes out of commission for the rest of the session. Everyone else heads east in search of the air genasi, and they end up finding her. Before the mistake can be corrected, a group of child thieves attempt to make way with the product. In a chase sequence which drags everyone across town and almost ends with one of the children dead, the group manages to retrieve the item and watch a new dawn at the [[Giskenroc/Locations/index|Bask]].
